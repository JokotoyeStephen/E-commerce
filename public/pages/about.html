About Kata Kara

Nigeria's Marketplace
Built for Everyone

"Kata Kara" — Yoruba for buying and selling. We are Nigeria's most trusted marketplace, connecting buyers and sellers across all 36 states.

Our Mission

To empower every Nigerian — whether in Lagos, Kano, or Enugu — to buy and sell freely, safely, and profitably. We believe commerce should be accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Vision

To become Africa's largest and most trusted digital marketplace — where millions of products, sellers, and buyers come together to create economic opportunity for all.
